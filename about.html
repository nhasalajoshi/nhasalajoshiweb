Nhasala Joshi trained as an electronics and communication engineer and began her career building hardware and prototypes, including IoT and automation products and research and development projects in a maker space. That hands-on background still shapes how she works: she prefers building something people can use to describing what should exist.

In 2015 she co-founded Women Leaders in Technology (WLiT), Nepal's pioneering women-in-tech nonprofit, first as Founding President and Program Lead and, since 2021, as Executive Director. Under her leadership WLiT has run 14 fellowship cohorts and built a community of more than 200 women tech professionals, while widening its work to include queer and trans communities, digital rights, and data.

Alongside WLiT, Nhasala advises and consults for Open Knowledge Nepal, where she initiated MulCAM. She is a multi-stakeholder group member of Youth IGF Nepal and a member of the Internet Society Nepal Chapter, and she has helped lead GDG Kathmandu and Women Techmakers events that drew more than a thousand attendees.

Nhasala does not believe technology is neutral, and she works to make it more just.
